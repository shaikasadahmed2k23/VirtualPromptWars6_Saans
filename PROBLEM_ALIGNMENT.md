# Problem alignment

## The brief

Build a GenAI assistant that helps people get through severe winter smog. It should **suggest low-emission daily routines**, **give indoor air-quality hacks** and **suggest sustainable alternatives**. Each suggestion should be **tailored to the user's location and lifestyle**, and the assistant should **explain the direct impact** of each choice.

Saans narrows this to families in Indian cities, where the winter NAQI often reaches Very poor or Severe, and children, older adults and people with heart or lung conditions are most at risk.

## Worked example

A real request to the live deployment at 19:20 IST on 2026-11-19 (`generatedAt: 2026-11-19T13:50:53Z`). The response came back as HTTP 200 with `source: "ai"`.

**Profile:** Delhi (city search), 2 adults and 2 children, sensitive members: yes. Two-wheeler, 12 km each way. Office 09:00–18:00, 5 days a week. 30 min of exercise in the morning. No purifier. LPG cooking. English.

**Air facts** (computed in [air-snapshot.ts](server/src/domain/air-snapshot.ts), [naqi.ts](server/src/domain/naqi.ts) and [windows.ts](server/src/domain/windows.ts)):

| Fact                  | Value                                          | Source function                    |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| NAQI now (19:00)      | 312, Very poor, dominant PM2.5                 | `computeNaqi`                      |
| PM2.5 now             | 135 µg/m³                                      | `buildAirSnapshot` (`currentPm25`) |
| Mean PM2.5, next 24 h | 117.6 µg/m³ (mean of 24 hourly values, 117.61) | `buildAirSnapshot` (`dayMeanPm25`) |
| Worst 2 h window      | today 20:00–22:00, mean PM2.5 149, NAQI 323    | `findWorstWindow`                  |
| Best 2 h window       | tomorrow 14:00–16:00, mean PM2.5 57, NAQI 95   | `findBestWindow`                   |

**Impacts.** Each is computed in `deriveImpacts` ([candidate-impacts.ts](server/src/plan/candidate-impacts.ts)) with `exposureAvoided` and `co2Saved` ([impact.ts](server/src/domain/impact.ts)) and the constants in [exposure.ts](shared/src/constants/exposure.ts) and [emissions.ts](shared/src/constants/emissions.ts). The commute takes 12 km × 2 trips ÷ 20 km/h = 1.2 h per office day.

| Recommendation (title from our copy)     | Arithmetic                                                  | Shown in the plan  |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Take the metro on office days            | 117.6 × 1.2 h × (1.0 m³/h × 1.0 − 0.5 m³/h × 0.6) = 98.8 µg | 98.8 µg (70 %)     |
|                                          | CO₂: 12 km × 10 trips × (0.04129 − 0.02929) kg/km = 1.44 kg | 1.44 kg CO₂ / week |
| Wear an N95 on your commute              | 117.6 × 1.2 h × 1.0 m³/h × (1 − 0.1) = 127.0 µg             | 127 µg (90 %)      |
| Exercise in the cleanest window          | 0.5 h × 2.5 m³/h × (149 − 57) = 115 µg                      | 115 µg (62 %)      |
| Keep windows shut at the peak            | 149 × 0.5 m³/h × 2 h × (1 − 0.6) = 59.6 µg                  | 59.6 µg (40 %)     |
| Consider a HEPA purifier for the bedroom | 117.6 × 0.5 m³/h × 8 h × (0.6 − 0.25) = 164.6 µg            | 164.6 µg (58 %)    |

"≈ N hours of breathing today's air at rest" is the avoided dose ÷ 135 ÷ 0.5 (`restHoursEquivalent`): 1.5, 1.9, 1.7, 0.9 and 2.4 hours, as returned.

**Words from Gemini.** The summary mentions the sensitive members, the missing purifier and the children, and advises a doctor's visit. That is REQ-08 at work:

> The air quality is currently very poor. The best time to be outdoors tomorrow is between 14:00 and 16:00. Since you have sensitive members in your household, please consult your doctor about their specific needs. As you do not have an air purifier, keeping windows closed is especially important for your children's health.

The routine had three blocks: avoid outdoor activity today 20:00–22:00, commute and office hours tomorrow 09:00–18:00, and a ventilation window tomorrow 14:00–16:00. There were two indoor hacks ("Seal gaps", "Wet mopping") and two alternatives ("Commute shift", "N95 mask"). Every number above came from the functions named, not from the model.
